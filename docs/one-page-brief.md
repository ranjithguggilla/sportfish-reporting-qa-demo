# One-page brief (three panels)

Use this structure for a slide, PDF, or the printable HTML page at `docs/reviewer-one-pager.html`.

## Problem

- Volunteer trip + tag notes often arrive incomplete or inconsistent.
- Staff time gets burned cleaning spreadsheets instead of reviewing real questions in the data.
- Reporters engage more when they see what their submission produced.

## Prototype

- Submit trip, catches, optional tag notes.
- Automatic checks surface likely GPS, count, date, or duplicate problems.
- Analyst queue with actions (review / escalate / dismiss).
- Downloads as CSV / GeoJSON + QA summary counts.
- Participant views: KPI cards, simple map footprint, timelines, tag history.

## Where this kind of tool helps

- Cleaner records at the door.
- Faster reviews with a visible queue.
- Easier exports for maps and spreadsheets.
- Better visibility for contributors.

Honest wording: workflow software—not a replacement for population science or official fishery decisions.
